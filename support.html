Common questions

My meals aren't appearing on my other device.
Sync uses your own iCloud account. Check that both devices are signed into the same Apple Account, and that iCloud Drive is on in Settings → your name → iCloud. Sync happens in the background, so give it a moment after you leave the app.
A barcode wasn't found.
Coverage varies a lot by country — European products are well covered, others less so. When nothing is found, the app offers to read the pack's own nutrition table from a photo. Point the camera at the per-100 g column and fill the frame with it. The result is remembered for that barcode, so the next scan is instant.
The nutrition it read from my photo looks wrong.
Any reading can be wrong. Every value is editable before you log it — fix it on the log sheet and the corrected figures are what get saved.
Voice or photo logging says it can't reach the server.
Those features need a connection. If you're online and it persists, email us — please say roughly when it happened, which helps us find it.
How do I delete my account and data?
Profile → Delete Account. It permanently removes your profile, meals, weight history and saved products from the device and from your iCloud, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. No email required.
Is this medical advice?
No. Gentle Eats gives estimates for general wellness and is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian.
