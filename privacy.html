Privacy Policy

Gentle Eats · last updated 29 September 2026

Gentle Eats is a food and nutrition tracker for iPhone. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, where it goes, and what it never does. It is written to match what the app actually does, not what would be convenient to claim.

The short version. Your meals and profile live on your device and in your own iCloud. We run no database of your food. When you log by voice, photo or text, that one recording, image or sentence is sent to our server and on to OpenAI to work out what the food was — and is not kept afterwards. We sell nothing, show no ads, and do not track you across other apps.

Your iCloud data is in your own Apple account. We cannot read it, and deleting your account removes it.

Voice, photos and typed meals

When you log a meal by voice, by photographing food or a nutrition label, or by typing it, that content is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed to OpenAI to identify the food and estimate its nutrition. We do not store the audio or the image after the result comes back, and OpenAI does not use API content to train its models. The resulting food entry is saved only where your other meals are: your device and your iCloud.

Barcodes

Scanning a barcode sends the barcode digits — nothing else, and nothing that identifies you — to Open Food Facts, an open food database. If a product isn't found, the app offers to read the pack's own nutrition label from a photo you take; that photo is handled exactly as described above, and the result is cached on your device against that barcode so the next scan is instant and works offline.

Sign-in

Signing in with Apple or Google gives us a stable identifier for your account and nothing more. The app requests only your name, and does not ask for your email address. Our server exchanges that identifier for its own session token, which is what fair-use limits are counted against.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, the app reads your active energy (calories burned) so your daily target reflects what you actually did. It requests read access only, never write access, and that figure stays on your device — it is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Deleting your account

Open Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, every logged meal, your weight history and your saved products from the device and from your iCloud, clears your stored credentials, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. It cannot be undone, and it does not require emailing anyone.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We show no advertising and use no advertising networks.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We use no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

Gentle Eats is not intended for children under 13, and setup will not accept a date of birth under that age.

Not medical advice

Gentle Eats provides estimates for general wellness. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. Nutrition figures are estimates — from public food databases, from a photographed label, or from an AI model — and can be wrong.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published here and in the app.
